# My stories — the STAR bank

**Yours.** Seven stories from your own projects, for "tell me about a time…" and "walk me through something you built". Claude never writes a story here. It may tighten your wording if you ask.

- **One story answers many questions.** A migration that went wrong is a failure story, a deadline story and a trade-off story. Prepare 5–7 well and reuse them.
- **Action is the long part.** Say what *you* did, not what the team did, and name the exact Apex, Flow, limit or setting.
- **Result has a number.** Records, hours, errors, users, money. If you do not know one, find out.
- **Power words last.** 3–5 words that bring the whole story back, the same way your note recall lines work.

Friday's drill in `npm run today` picks the first empty slot. Say the story out loud in under two minutes.

Q: What are the two platform building blocks for custom agent actions?
A: Invocable Apex methods (@InvocableMethod) and autolaunched Flows.

Q: Why do an action's input/output descriptions matter so much?
A: They ARE the agent's understanding of the tool — the reasoning engine picks and fills actions based on them. Write them like prompts.
